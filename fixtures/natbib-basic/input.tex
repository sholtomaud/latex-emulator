\section{Citations}
Energy systems were described by \citet{odum_energy_1996}, and later
refined \citep[see][p.~10, ch.~2]{odum_energy_1996}. Two authors are
cited as \citet{brown_emergy_2004}; three or more as
\citet{hall_energy_2014}. Several works can be cited at once
\citep{brown_emergy_2004,hall_energy_2014}. The author alone is
\citeauthor{odum_energy_1996} and the year alone is
\citeyear{odum_energy_1996}. A plain \cite{odum_energy_1996} behaves
like \verb|\citet|, and a page note alone gives
\citep[p.~10]{odum_energy_1996}.
